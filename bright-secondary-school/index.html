Mission

To nurture learners who are academically capable, practically skilled and morally upright, with a place for every child.

Motto

Light for every child.

Vision

A school where every child, including those with disabilities, learns, grows and leads.

Headteacher’s message

Welcome to Bright Secondary School. We are a new school, built by SAVE THE CHILD, and we are proud to open our doors to learners who deserve the best start.

Our teachers will combine strong academics with practical training in farming, computing and vocational trades. Every learner will be known by name, supported when they struggle and encouraged to lead. We invite parents and guardians to walk with us from the first day.

NAKABANGO RESTY
Headteacher
