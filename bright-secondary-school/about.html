About us

How Bright Secondary School began, what we stand for and who guides us.

Origin Mission and vision Governance

Our origin

SAVE THE CHILD, a non-governmental organisation that works for children’s welfare, saw that many families in this suburb had no quality, affordable secondary school within reach.

After consulting parents and local leaders, the organisation secured land and began building Bright Secondary School. The plan goes beyond classrooms: dormitories, a computer laboratory, a school farm, a kindergarten, a vocational section and a home for children with disabilities.

Mission and vision

Mission

To nurture learners who are academically capable, practically skilled and morally upright, with a place for every child.

Vision

A school where every child, including those with disabilities, learns, grows and leads.

Core values

Care, inclusion, discipline, honesty and service to the community.
