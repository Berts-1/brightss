Admissions

Everything a parent or guardian needs to apply to Bright Secondary School.

How to apply

  1. Read the fees and rules
    Review them below with your child.
  2. Fill in the online form
    Complete every required field.
  3. Wait for our call
    The office contacts you within five working days.
  4. Visit the school
    Bring report cards and a birth certificate for verification.

School rules and regulations

All learners and parents agree to these rules when they join.

Rule 1
Learners attend school every day, on time and in the full uniform.
Rule 2
Respect for teachers, staff and fellow learners is expected at all times.
Rule 3
Bullying, fighting, theft and drug use are not tolerated and lead to disciplinary action.
Rule 4
Mobile phones, weapons and electronic gadgets are not allowed on campus without permission.
Rule 5
Boarders must remain within school grounds and follow the dormitory timetable.
Rule 6
Learners care for school property, farm tools and laboratory equipment.
Rule 7
Fees are paid by the date set for each term; late payment must be discussed with the Bursar.
Rule 8
Parents and guardians attend school meetings and collect learners only with a signed permission.
